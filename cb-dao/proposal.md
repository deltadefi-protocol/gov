## Why Cardano needs a Hydra-based orderbook DEX

Cardano's DeFi ecosystem benefits from a venue that can deliver reliable price discovery and deep liquidity for core pairs (especially ADA↔stable). A Hydra-based orderbook DEX fills this gap by enabling high-throughput, low-latency trading while keeping settlement anchored to Cardano, which is critical for attracting programmatic traders and market makers. Stronger market quality (tighter spreads, deeper near-mid liquidity, lower slippage) improves trading efficiency across the ecosystem and supports sustainable fee-based activity that can persist through market cycles. The non-custodial feature of DeltaDeFi also help it to differentiate from centralized exchanges.

## Why a Hydra-based orderbook DEX can still win in a down market

In a risk-off market, users and integrators stop chasing subsidized yields and start demanding execution quality and reliability: tight spreads, predictable slippage, fast settlement, and clear risk controls. This is where an orderbook DEX has a structural edge — it competes on market quality, not on emissions-driven APR.

DeltaDeFi adds a second advantage by building the orderbook on Hydra, which enables high-throughput, low-latency trading while keeping settlement anchored to Cardano. In bad markets, liquidity is thin. If quoting is costly, market makers widen spreads. Our programmatic access and zero fee model on order placement and cancellation reduce quote friction and enables tighter spreads and deeper liquidity even in risk-off markets.

## Why the orderbook model fits "risk-off" conditions

- **Capital efficiency near the mid-price:** liquidity can be placed exactly where trading happens (depth close to mid), improving execution without requiring excessive TVL.
- **Transparent market quality:** spreads, depth, and slippage are directly measurable, making it easier for serious traders and market makers to evaluate and commit liquidity.
- **Programmatic participation:** an API-first venue attracts market makers, bots, and integrators who continue trading through market cycles, supporting consistent volume and real fee revenue.
